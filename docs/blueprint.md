# **App Name**: GartenMeister

## Core Features:

- Garden Plot Design: Allows to design herb beds in a garden plot of 87x43 meters, supporting parallel arrangement and variable widths with 0.5-meter spacing.
- Bed Number Management: Manages numbering of beds from 1 to 30, preventing reuse of assigned numbers unless manually cleared.
- Herb Variety Database: Enables entry of herb varieties in a table, including fixed varieties (Thymian, Oregano, etc.) and the ability to add new varieties and sub-varieties.
- Special Bed Designations: Provides options to designate beds as 'Blühstreifen' (flowering strip) or 'Brachfläche' (fallow land) with specific details like occupancy date, notes, and harvest date.
- Experimental Bed Configuration: Supports creation of 'Versuchsbeete' (experimental beds) with variable lengths and widths, including detailed planting information similar to standard herb beds.
- Harvest Data Tracking: Records harvest data for each bed and generates kilo yield reports for each herb variety, factoring in the percentage of productive plants to account for losses.
- Graphical Bed Representation: Represents beds graphically, showing bed number, herb type, plant count, and productive plant count, with color-coded display and PDF export option.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft green (#8FBC8F) to reflect the natural and calming aspect of gardening.
- Background color: Light green (#E0EEE0), offering a subtle, fresh backdrop.
- Accent color: Earthy brown (#A0522D) for actionable elements, providing contrast and grounding.
- Use clear and readable typography to ensure effortless interaction, particularly when inputting and reviewing critical gardening data.
- Employ simple, intuitive icons for various herbs, bed types, and actions within the app. Represent different actions through corresponding simple icons.
- Maintain a clean, organized layout with a focus on usability. Group related functions together and use clear visual cues to guide users.
